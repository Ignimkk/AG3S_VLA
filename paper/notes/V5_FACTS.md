## 2. What each option does mechanically (V5 = T39 E3b + these). Sources: --help strings benchmark/trajopt/serve_safe.py (L1035-1075, 1187-1196, 1236-1243, 1280-1305), impl docs benchmark/ag3s/docs/handoff/T41a.impl.md, T41c.impl.md, T43R.impl.md, T43R2.impl.md, T43Q.impl.md, T43T.impl.md §6, code pointers below. V5 SETS (7 items): (1) --no-subtask-gate (2) --obstacle-margin 0.010 (3) client --safe-hold-mode measured (4) --held-body-cover (5) --closing-keep-geometry (6) --latched-identity-hold (7) --supported-max-bottom-mm 60. (--obstacle-policy-hidden is client-only and used only in V5B.) The user's list mentions R and Q: they are NOT part of V5 (they are V2/V3/V6; R dropped for over-conservativeness + real-time collapse: V2 TO 2065 ms, grasp 0/8; Q (V3) hurts grasp: V3 vs V4 grasp 0 vs 8 discordant, p=0.0078).

(1) `--no-subtask-gate` — [CODE serve_safe.py L1240; LOG SUBTASK-g]. After T39 the subtask probe (pi0.5 prefix V-cache of layer 4 -> pick/place/home label) + gate became default ON (bf65756, 2026-10-04): under label place/home a new target adoption is blocked and home after PLACED releases the carve. The V chain passes --no-subtask-gate = "label recorded only, decisions independent of the label" = the T39 behaviour. So V5's grounding/destination logic is still the T39 attention-leader/non-graspable-destination logic, NOT the subtask gate. [Check: the T43 chain refuses start unless the log shows gate off? Not verified; the flag is in p5/start_server.sh E3B string]. Task/phase for V5: phase injected `approach`, manipulator injected (left; ep1834 right).
(2) `--obstacle-margin 0.010` (T41 a) — [CODE trajopt/linearize.py L684-790 `_obstacle_rows`, config L591-593; T41a.impl.md]. TO ESDF clearance rows are d - r - m >= 0. With margin on, m is raised to max(esdf_margin, 10 mm) ONLY for robot-sphere rows whose nearest surface is neither the manipulated target (|d - d_object| <= 1 voxel), nor a support plane (|d - plane| <= 2 voxel), nor a held-object query row. Query spheres are not enlarged (user instruction), so it is "10 mm of extra clearance from non-target, non-support obstacles only". T39: margin 0 for all rows (esdf-margin 0). Rows against the table/apple keep margin 0 so the hand can still reach the fruit. (Pre-T43 test: at 10 mm hurdle round C 1/5 -> 2/5, D 1/4 -> 2/4 [LOG T41a].) Interaction known: margin on a row near the apple during closing pushed fingers away (T43 U) -> motivation for (5).
(3) client `--safe-hold-mode measured` (T41 c) — [CODE pi05_infer.py L791,1372; T41c.impl.md]. When the verdict is HOLD (client executes no policy row) the T39 mode `fixed` commanded the last commanded arm target (q_hold = last command at HOLD entry); `measured` latches the MEASURED joint pose at HOLD entry and commands that (continuing HOLD chunks keep the first latch; re-latch only after a chunk executed). Gripper command in both = last command (commanding the measured gripper opening drops the apple within 2 steps: T41c.impl table). Reason: arm lagged the command by 7.3 deg and kept drifting into the bar during HOLD (C1982). Server verdict logic unchanged.
(4) `--held-body-cover` (T43 R2) — [CODE ag3s/robot_models/held_object.py `body_cover_spheres`, runtime/pipeline.py L281,776, `_frame_held_attached`; T43R2.impl.md]. The held fruit's TO query spheres are rebuilt to cover the whole body: fitted sphere + a bottom sphere of the same radius lowered to the support plane (+5 mm slip), spheres for observed points outside both (merge rule H2), segment spheres along the horizontal axis for elongated fruit (banana/pear); count <= max_attached_primitives (4). The "J1 lift" (raising the fitted sphere off the support so it does not read the table band) is applied only while the body is within support+band+10 mm (current pose or the chunk's reference window), not during carry. Distance field / self-filter / TSDF-free volume still use the old (off) spheres: only TO's queries change. T43 S had measured the T39-style held sphere covering only 43-64 % of the apple mesh (bottom contact 3.6-15.5 mm outside). With cover: <= 1.1 mm outside (off 6.8-16.3), TO held row <= 0 in 8/8 contact replays. Residual (stated in impl): new negative rows near support (7 of 129 runs), at place against crate WALLS (17 of 19 new negatives), closed-loop effect then unmeasured.
(5) `--closing-keep-geometry` (T43 T2 step 2) — [CODE ag3s/stages/target_grounding.py L141, L1169-1176 (reason CLOSING_KEPT_GEOMETRY); pipeline.py L1417]. While the grasp latch is `closing` (frozen, nothing attached) the Target's geometry (point set, centroid, hence the target-free carve-out ball and the later attach snapshot) stays the pre-closing geometry instead of being replaced by the (partial, finger-occluded) cluster of that frame. T30 had let geometry follow observation under the premise "the object moves with the hand", false during closing. Measured cause: first closing chunk 114->33 points, carve ball 59.6->39.9 mm (T42-1 ep1908); V4 closing carve ball median 50.0 mm (min 33.6) vs V5 55.0 mm (min 53.2) [VERIFY-JSON focus]. Effect claimed: fingers no longer pushed out of the apple (replay: pushed >2 mm 9 -> 0 of 34 closing chunks), permanent HOLD family A 32/32 chunks released in replay; side effects stated: attach-held spheres larger (24 of 92 attaches >= 50 mm single sphere), new negative held rows on 13 recorded chunks.
(6) `--latched-identity-hold` (T43 T 3(a)) — [CODE target_grounding.py L695, L1199 `_latched_keeps`, pipeline.py L187, L463]. While the grasp latch is LATCHED/CLOSING (a pick target locked) a challenger frame (another cluster leads attention) counts toward a Target switch only with evidence: (i) the in-place cluster is no longer the held target (association_rejected) AND the hand is away from it (> hand_occlusion_reach), or (ii) the hand went to the challenger (T32b). "Not visible", merged, or score slipping are treated as gaps like while frozen. Fix for C/D1982 where wrist-camera attention peak moved onto the bar and the Target switched apple -> bar after the apple was hand-occluded. In replay: apple kept seq 30-75, switch count 0.
(7) `--supported-max-bottom-mm 60` (T43 T 3(c)) — [CODE target_grounding.py L709, L1470-1500 `_support_test`, ag3s/stages/admissibility.py L59]. A candidate cluster is `not_supported` (non-admissible as Target) if its lowest point is higher than 60 mm above the nearest support plane and it is not inside the hand's occlusion reach, or its highest point is below `supported_min_top` (10 mm). Rationale values from 263 adoptions: fruit on table bottom <= 45.1 mm; obstacle pieces (hurdle bar top, crate rim) 76.8-177 mm; carried fruit has hand gap 2.5-10.8 mm vs >= 56.8 mm for obstacle pieces. Not run while frozen or when no support plane. (The hurdle FOOT on the table is not rejected by this rule.)
Not in V5 (for method text): (R) `--gripper-cover`: adds inscribed cover spheres on palm/fingers (greedy set cover of the collision-mesh surface, radius <= current max per link, never beyond the grasping face) -> palm coverage 16 -> 99.9 %, fingers 35 -> 99.8 %, spheres 122 -> 1594, TO 41-59 -> 224-264 ms. (Q) `--servo-model`: TO predicts the robot's actual pose with a per-joint linear PD servo model derived from the MJCF (kp 1500/2000, kv 245-303, damping 30, armature 10; velocity estimated from exec_feedback) and adds clearance rows at the predicted pose at the end of each control row; `--sweep-check` adds clearance rows at intermediate (interpolated or servo-predicted) poses so the query spheres move <= --sweep-max-mm between checks (V3: 24 mm, <=4 samples, 6 rows/step). (target-volume-exempt) rows whose nearest surface point lies inside the carve ball are treated as target rows (no obstacle margin, excluded from sweep rows); replay showed no difference to (5). (refused-identity-release, held-free-observed-mm) not used (flag-only: release identity after N rejection frames; clear TSDF voxels at the pre-grasp object location while held).

## 4. Does V5 change the Method description? (per part)
 - Grounding / Target identity: YES, in the identity-maintenance layer only. Attention->3-D->cluster->graspable Target selection (L8H2, attention-seeded clusters, destination exclusion) is as in T39 (subtask gate OFF). New: (a) admissibility requires the cluster to rest on a support plane within 60 mm (or be in the hand) and top >= 10 mm above it; (b) while the grasp latch is LATCHED/CLOSING the Target is re-selected only on evidence (cluster gone + hand away, or hand moved to challenger), not on mere loss of visibility/attention shift; (c) during CLOSING the pre-closing geometry is kept. All three use the grasp latch (state inferred from executed commands + measured gripper opening, as in T39) so the Method must describe latch states searching/latched/closing/held/placed [grasp_state_counts in verify.json use these names].
 - Target-free layer (ball): same mechanism (ball around the Target removed from the fine ESDF layer above table, queried by authorised gripper links), but its size is now stable through closing (median carve radius 55.0 mm, range 53.2-55.1 in V5 vs V4 50.0, min 33.6). target-volume-exempt (row-level exemption) is NOT on.
 - Attach / held geometry: YES. Attach snapshot = pre-closing geometry (not the partial closing-frame cluster); held query spheres = fitted sphere + bottom sphere to the support (+5 mm slip) + residual-point spheres / segment spheres, <= 4 (R2), J1 lift only near support. Field-side held volume (self-filter, TSDF free) unchanged.
 - Clearance rows: YES (one addition): obstacle-only 10 mm margin (max(esdf_margin, 0.010) for rows whose nearest surface is not target/support/held). Everything else (122 gripper spheres, d - r - margin >= 0 linearised rows, margin 0 for the rest) as T39. Method equation must carry a per-row margin m_i in {0, 10 mm} by row class.
 - SQP: NO change for V5 (3-iteration SQP/OSQP, same cost/limits, waypoint rows only). Servo model / sweep rows are not in V5 (that is Q; V3/V6 only; V3 hurt grasping). TO solve median 61.5 ms / p90 138.9 ms (V5/H, 845 chunks) but round trip median 1145.7 ms.
 - Gate / HOLD: MINOR/YES. Verdict reason kinds unchanged (V5 HOLD kinds: collision 162, unverified 9). Client HOLD freezes at the measured arm pose (not last command); gripper last command. Subtask gate OFF (as T39). HOLD counts are larger in V5 (163 chunks / 6 runs vs V4 56 / 5): 97 of these are trailing HOLD after place in ep1807 (two seeds); 148 chunks have the worst row on a held query sphere with negative clearance (4 runs). V5 therefore has more conservative HOLD residue despite higher success; the files give no safety interpretation.
 - Evaluation protocol difference: obstacle scene (P2 overbar) with obstacle visible in policy images; stop at contact (stop-distance 0); success measured on hurdle runs; not the T39 clear scene. Different task conditions => V5 numbers are NOT comparable to T39 E0/E3b 30/48 vs 29/48.
