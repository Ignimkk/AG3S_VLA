Figure 1 (variant A, performance teaser), CVPR page 1, full text width (about 1.6:1). Follow the reference image closely. Clean academic style: white background, photographic panels with thin coloured frames, rounded colour bands behind rows, bold sans-serif labels, no icons, no drop shadows.

Message: a frozen vision-language-action (VLA) policy alone collides when the target sits next to an obstacle or a hurdle blocks the carry path; with AG3S the same policy picks and places successfully.

Main area (left three quarters), a 2 x 2 grid of photographic frames of a white-and-black dual-arm robot at a wooden table with a basket, a red apple (the target), a pear beside it, and a red arch-shaped bar (the hurdle) standing over the edge of the basket. All four frames show the same scene and the same hurdle.
- Column headers: "Pick: obstacle next to the target" and "Place: hurdle on the carry path".
- Row 1 on a light red band, row label "VLA only" in red, written vertically.
  Left: the gripper reaches for the apple and hits the red bar (a red glow marks the contact; a red line shows the hand's path). Badge: "x collides -> pick fails".
  Right: the hand carries the apple straight into the hurdle bar (red glow, red path). Badge: "x hits the hurdle -> place fails".
- Row 2 on a light gold band, row label "VLA + AG3S (ours)" in dark gold.
  Left: the apple is grasped and lifted next to the hurdle without touching it (gold path). Badge: "check avoids, then grasps".
  Right: a gold path arcs up and over the hurdle into the basket while the apple is carried. Badge: "check clears the hurdle, places".
Failure badges are red, success badges green. Frames of row 2 have gold borders.
