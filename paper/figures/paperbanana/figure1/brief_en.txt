Clean academic style: white background, photographic panels with thin light-grey frames, flat colour chips, sans-serif labels in sentence case, no drop shadows, no icons, no extra robots or objects.

Message: a frozen vision-language-action (VLA) policy grasps the instructed fruit even though another fruit touches it, because its own attention is lifted into a 3D target whose boundary decides where the fingers may make contact.

Top line, centred, italic: "Put the pear in the basket."
