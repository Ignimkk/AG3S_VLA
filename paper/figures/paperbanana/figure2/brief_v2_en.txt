Title: "AG3S: one frozen VLA decides what may be touched, by which link, and when".

Top strip, the framework (light grey card). "RGB-D + instruction" -> "frozen VLA (no retraining)", which emits three outputs: "actions" (blue), "attention" (gold), "hidden state" (orange).
- attention -> gold box "3D target from attention (no segmenter)" with a small gold tag "C1 What".
- hidden state -> orange box "object role: target -> attached -> released" with an orange tag "C3 When".
- Both feed one large gold box, the visual centre of the strip, tagged "C2 Who": "link-specific contact representation", with two typeset lines:
  d_l(x) = d^free(x) if l is in L_contact(phi)
  d_l(x) = d^scene(x) otherwise
  and a small italic note "free = scene minus the target's own observed extent".
- The gold box -> "CCR (SQP)" -> "execute or hold". A blue lane "nominal action chunk" runs from "actions" across the top into CCR.

Bottom, three equal tall cards, one per contribution. Each card has a title, an italic one-line subtitle, a red "Prior:" row with one photo and a red badge, then a gold "AG3S:" row with two or three photos and short captions.

  AG3S: the policy's own attention -> 3D. Photos: "frozen-VLA attention" (warm heat glow on the pear) and "lifted 3D target" (pear covered by orange points; touching orange covered by blue points).

  Prior: global target exclusion. Photo: gold gripper over two fruits; a translucent red ball removes all voxels inside it, including part of the neighbour. Badge "x freed for every link; neighbour erased".

C3 "When it changes" (orange card), subtitle "the object's role follows the task phase".
  Prior: fixed object role. Photo: the robot carrying a red apple near a red arch-shaped bar; red glow around the apple. Badge "x held object not modelled".

Three photos: "target: fingers may touch" (gold finger spheres over voxels; the apple's voxels faint orange), "attached: avoids the bar" (the apple inside a translucent purple sphere on the hand), "released: obstacle again" (the apple in the basket covered again by blue voxels).
