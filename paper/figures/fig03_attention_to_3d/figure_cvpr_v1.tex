\caption{\textbf{Method overview.} A frozen VLA predicts the action chunk from three camera views, the instruction, and the robot state; the same forward pass exposes the noun attention map and the hidden state. \textbf{Target segmentation} (Sec.~\ref{sec:grounding}) grows the attention seeds along depth into the target's observed surface (Eq.~\eqref{eq:energy}), with no external segmenter. \textbf{Task stage recognition} (Sec.~\ref{sec:phase}) classifies the stage of the task from the hidden state, and the grasp state follows from gripper feedback. The \textbf{contact-aware collision map} (Sec.~\ref{sec:contact}) gives the contact links a map from which the target surface is removed and every other link the full scene; once the object is grasped it joins the robot. \textbf{Action refinement} (Sec.~\ref{sec:refine}) refines the chunk under Eq.~\eqref{eq:clearance} and executes it or holds. The bottom strip shows one simulated episode stage by stage.}
