\paragraph{Limitations.}
\label{sec:limitations}
ATS depends on the selected attention head and the separability of objects in depth. Its geometric exclusion region
can include nearby obstacle surfaces, and its graspability and destination heuristics are tailored to the current
pick-and-place setting. HSR uses a closed three-class label set. The present query geometry covers gripper links and
attached-object approximations, with no self-collision checking. Waypoint checks and observed geometry do not cover
all intermediate motion or unseen surfaces. The current implementation uses an additional attention-extraction pass;
end-to-end latency and behavior under sensor noise require evaluation. \tbd{Add a source-linked failure example
and measured operating limits after the final evaluation.}
